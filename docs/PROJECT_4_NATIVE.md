## Native adaptation decisions

Unit 3’s broad/dated nutrition and treatment claims are not converted into health recommendations. New graded language uses body meanings, relative clauses, should/might forms and So/Neither auxiliaries. Its independent rehearsal story concerns checking props and asking for help with a heavy box. The original doctor scripts remain clearly attributed language scenes. There is no generated medical dosing exercise.

Dated environment population figures and extinction predictions are not presented as current facts in new readings. New passive practice describes an imaginary workshop, explicit task charts and fictional plans. The process chart is an original model-handling sequence, not a claim about real-world factory practice. Duration diagrams use a fixed fictional reference date of 1 January 2030 so their answers do not depend on the real clock.

Body-part dots, clothing patterns, sleeves and timelines are original SVG components; process steps and tables use native HTML. Reused independent generated atlases were visually inspected: evolve-3-native/city-materials-v1.png (eight full material/object compounds), evolve-1-native/actions.png (drawing, painting, guitar and swimming), and evolve-5-native/exploration-nature-v1.png (forest, frog and shark). The eight Project 4 material pointers refine their crop rectangles for the atlas's unequal lower rows, removing neighbouring-tile strips while retaining original IDs, file hashes and unchanged image bytes. No bitmap was generated or edited for this checkpoint. No textbook-page image enters gameplay.

Overlapping word senses have separated distractors: baggy/loose, general/specific upper-body garments, trousers/jeans, nervous/worried, decision/choice and decide/choose. All vocabulary cards remain available; the overlapping general choices are excluded from pair modes. Relative-pronoun questions never mark otherwise-valid that/which alternatives wrong by offering both as incompatible answers.
