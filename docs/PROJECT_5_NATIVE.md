## Independent adaptation decisions

The supplied-edition memories, school account, insect-food discussion, proposed space hotel, train announcements, historical correction text and country-comparison check are marked as source-period material. Their claims, forecasts, prices and travel details are not asserted as current information or personal advice.

The source’s Cumbre Vieja catastrophe hypothesis, dated population/diet claims, blanket statements about computer games and historical superlatives are not presented as independently verified facts in new readings. Native models use an imaginary island, a fictional museum role, a community film plan and fictional travel tables. Medical and warning words are language meanings; no medicine dose or personalized care exercise is generated. The Harvard/Yale material’s changing counts and the country check’s uncertain superlatives are not new factual quizzes. Track 29 review avoids uncertain dates and obsolete racial labels.

Personal qualities are not inferred from appearance. New descriptions use behaviour clues, and bullying comprehension concerns the character recognizing and stopping mistreatment. Civic language is taught through vocabulary and a fictional student-council discussion. The downtown-plan recording stays a fictional language scene. Job applications remain clearly fictional, local, unscored writing practice with no real contact details or submission.

Reflexive pronouns, second conditionals, so ... that, question forms, gerunds, separable phrasal verbs, modal passives, reporting pronouns and indirect word order retain explicit grammatical cues and unique choices. Valid relative-pronoun and conventional-reporting alternatives are not offered as mutually exclusive wrong answers. Overlapping vocabulary senses are separated in choice/pair pools while all cards remain available.

## Native art and shared photo correction

Object problems are original inline SVGs. Clocks, calendars, process cards and tables use the existing native components. Photo choices reuse reviewed tiles from Evolve 2 objects, Evolve 3 city scenes, Evolve 5 nature/space and Evolve 1 actions. No bitmap is generated or edited, and no book-page image enters gameplay.

The shared photo renderer now shuffles image options once per question and labels their displayed positions A/B/C. The old canonical text A no longer reveals the correct picture. Grading still uses each original option ID, while new attempts and correct-answer feedback name the image with its readable description. Order stays stable during load, retry and feedback. Older source packs and existing attempts are unchanged. Tests now select by image identity rather than assuming letter A is correct, and a dedicated regression checks grading and feedback for a correct image displayed at C.

Live asset resolution also refines the unequal lower rows of the reviewed city/materials atlas where an older pointer has no explicit crop. It preserves explicit crops, original image bytes and immutable release JSON. This corrects neighbouring-tile strips in earlier reused photos as well as retaining Project 4’s existing explicit refinements.
