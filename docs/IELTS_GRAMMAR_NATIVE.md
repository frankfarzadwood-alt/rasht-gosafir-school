## Accepted language and personal responses

Questions distinguish an explicit target form or meaning from other valid grammatical possibilities. Future forms can overlap; reported-speech backshift can depend on context. May/might/could remain valid possibilities. Defining subject relatives can allow who/that. Singular and plural agreement after suitable neither/either-of and none-of phrases is acknowledged. Bare neither/either + singular noun uses singular agreement. Zero-article and complete slash-separated answer sets are excluded from single-gap tile insertion.

The twenty-five readings and writing prompts are independently authored skills models aligned to their units. They do not reproduce every source reading passage, original IELTS question type or official writing task. A/B/C/D labels preserve learning structure while all graded controls stay native. Writing is a separate self-review workspace; speaking is a timed rehearsal with no automated voice or band grading.

Writing keys are english-quest-writing-ielts-grammar-65-unit-N. Speaking notes use english-quest-speaking-ielts-grammar-65-ieltsg.speaking.room. These are separate from prior books' drafts and objective progress. Personal tasks belong to the actual D sections; unrelated or listening-only pools cannot fall back to another book's task. Timers, self-checks and saved notes award no XP or mastery.
