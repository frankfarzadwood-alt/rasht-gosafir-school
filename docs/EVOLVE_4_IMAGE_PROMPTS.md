## Final prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: native English-learning game photo atlas. Generate ONE perfectly square image divided edge-to-edge into exactly 4 columns and 4 rows, sixteen equal square photographic tiles. No gutters, frames, labels, letters, numbers, logos, watermark, or book page design. Each tile is a separate clear, simple natural photograph with its subject centered and fully inside that tile, no overlap. Row 1 left to right: a whole pineapple with one cut yellow slice; one shiny purple eggplant; one dark green zucchini; a round green cabbage. Row 2: a garlic bulb and separate cloves; a fresh ginger root; several pink cooked shrimp; a whole silvery tuna fish on a fishmonger's plain ice counter. Row 3: rinsing leafy lettuce under running water in a kitchen sink; chopping a carrot with a knife on a cutting board; stirring soup with a spoon in a saucepan; boiling water with large visible bubbles in a pot. Row 4: frying an egg in a shallow frying pan with a little oil; barbecuing food on an outdoor charcoal grill; baking bread inside an open domestic oven; peeling an apple with a peeler. Clean food photography, realistic textures, bright diffuse daylight, plain uncluttered contexts, easy recognition at thumbnail size. The cooking actions should be distinct and safely depicted. Create original independent images, never a textbook screenshot. Make the full image square, each tile exactly one quarter of its width and height.
