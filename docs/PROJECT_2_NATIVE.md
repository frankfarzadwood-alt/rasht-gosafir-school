Project 2, fourth edition, is added to the same TypeScript/React/TanStack/Vite source. It is the eleventh of seventeen requested books. Continue with Project 3 after this checkpoint and create one cumulative Lovable source ZIP only after the whole collection. No new dependency, deployment or intermediate ZIP is needed.

## Native curriculum practice

- 831 graded activities and 300 vocabulary cards.
- Six main units, 24 named A–D lessons and six progress-check scopes. Existing `project-2.u001`–`.u006` and `.s1`–`.s4` identities are retained; the new `.s5` nodes are progress checks.
- An opening refresher (`project-2.introduction`, 22 activities) and final revision (`project-2.revision`, 34 activities). Neither is presented as a fabricated numbered source unit.
- 32 supplied exercise recordings and 190 native listening questions, including seven animal-sound identification questions.
- Six original fictional reading models with 24 questions; six unscored writing tasks with self-review criteria; 24 sentence builders, sixteen additional irregular-past questions and thirteen additional adverb-form questions.
- Eight native calendar questions, six analogue clocks, eight original weather/temperature diagrams, five native tables and eighteen photo-choice questions using existing independent generated art.
- Seven optional reviewed visual sets open the calendar, clocks, weather, animal photos, food photos, cooking photos and landscape photos directly from their corresponding lesson pages.

The curriculum covers daily routines, ordinals and dates, chores and frequency, farm animals and their young, wild animals, present continuous, subject/object pronouns, must, holidays and past forms, food and quantities, recipes, landscapes and dimensions, weather and compass points, comparatives/superlatives and as … as, programme/film types, going to, manner adverbs, have to and suggestions.

Activities are independently authored practice aligned to these source targets. This does not claim to reproduce or automatically grade every printed crossword, picture label, word search, personal response or appendix entry. Original fictional readings are labelled with their own provenance; they are not presented as printed source passages. No separate Project 2 test papers were supplied. Native progress checks are practice, without invented official marks or timings. Unsupported foundation test placeholders are hidden for the released Project books while their immutable planning IDs remain intact.
